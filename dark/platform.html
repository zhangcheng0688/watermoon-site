SLAM.SYSTEMS COR

the Sport Multimedia Playout System

One operator. One interface. Every screen, score, sponsor and light cue in the building — from pre-game intro to the last frame of the night.

Six modules. One system.

01

DIRECTOR

Real-time control of the whole show. DIRECTOR fires cues across screens, ribbons and lighting — live, from the booth or from an iPad on the sideline.

02

DECK

Playout and playlists without the drama. Build matchday playlists in Excel (.number/.xls), import, arrange, run — sponsor rotations included.

03

SCORE

Scoring and timing at professional level. Hockey, football, handball and more — official-grade score data on every display in the venue.

04

NERV

Players and synchronization. NERV keeps main screen, ribbons and scoreboards frame-synchronous, with main/backup redundancy built in.

05

CENTRO

Signal and device control. CENTRO routes live feeds, switches sources and commands the entire rack — one interface for every device.

06

CLOUD

Remote monitoring and OOHD 2027. CLOUD watches every system from anywhere, alerts before failure — and turns screens into ad inventory between matchdays.
